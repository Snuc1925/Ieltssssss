### #310 — Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of encouraging tourism in fragile ecosystems.

*Advantages / Disadvantages · 280 từ · điểm AI: 7.5*

**Rainforests, coral reefs, mountains and polar regions** attract **adventurous travellers**, but these ecosystems **recover slowly from damage**. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of encouraging tourism in **fragile ecosystems** before explaining my own view.

**On the positive side**, tourism in **fragile ecosystems** can **fund their protection**. **Entrance fees and local jobs** give people a reason to protect wildlife instead of **hunting or clearing land**, and visitors who see these places in person often become **supporters of conservation**. **A national park that charges visitors**, **for example**, can **pay rangers and research** that would otherwise be unaffordable. Where such controls are absent, tourism should not be encouraged.

**In conclusion**, tourism can pay for the protection of **fragile ecosystems**, but it can also destroy them. On balance, I believe that it should be encouraged only in small numbers, under strict control, and where the income supports conservation.
