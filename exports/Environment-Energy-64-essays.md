### #185 — Some people believe that climate change is mainly caused by human activity. Others think natural factors are more significant. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

*Discuss both views · 275 từ · điểm AI: 7.0*

**Opinions are divided as to whether** climate change is **mainly caused by human activity** or whether **natural factors** are more **significant**. This essay will consider both perspectives before explaining my own opinion.

**On the one hand**, those who **emphasise natural factors** point out that the climate has always changed through natural causes. **Volcanic eruptions**, **variations in the sun's energy** and **shifts in ocean currents** produced **warm periods and ice ages** long before humans existed. Some people therefore **argue** that today's warming may be **part of a natural cycle**, and that **human influence is exaggerated**. This view also reminds us that climate science is complex, and that scientists continue to study **natural variation** in order to **improve their predictions**.

**On the other hand**, supporters of the human-activity explanation **argue** that **scientific evidence** links the recent rise in temperatures to human activity. **Burning coal, oil and gas** and **clearing forests** release gases that **trap heat**, and temperatures have risen **alongside emissions** since **the industrial era** began. Natural cycles, which operate over thousands of years, cannot explain **such rapid warming**. A greenhouse works in a similar way, which is why these gases are said to **warm the planet**. **In addition**, studies of past climates show that the present rate of change is **unusually fast**.
